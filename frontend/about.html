Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science

Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science (MITS) is promoted by Muthoot M. George Institute of Technology, a Section 25 company within the Muthoot Group.

The institute focuses on providing quality technical education and developing students with strong academic and professional skills.

THE MUTHOOT GROUP

More than a century of legacy.

The Muthoot Group is a diversified business group with a legacy spanning more than a century. Its journey began in 1887 when Muthoot Ninan Mathai started a retail and wholesale grain business in Kozhencherry, Kerala.

Over the years, the group expanded into multiple sectors and established a strong presence across India and international markets.

The group places emphasis on values such as trust, transparency, customer satisfaction and responsible business practices. Its long-standing legacy and diversified operations form an important part of the background of Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science.
